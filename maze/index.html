On a keyboard you can move both at once — Erika with ←↑↓→, Brendan with WASD.
You can also swipe on the maze to move whoever's selected.

How to play
  • Erika starts on the left edge, Brendan on the right. Both need to reach the glowing heart in the centre.
  • It's two little puzzles sharing one maze — solve both to win.
  • Phone: tap a name to pick who you're moving, then use the arrows (or swipe on the maze).
  • Computer: steer both at the same time — arrow keys for Erika, WASD for Brendan.
  • Tap New maze any time for a fresh layout, or change the difficulty.
