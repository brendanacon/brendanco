Stay safe
  • Check with your GP before starting, especially with heart, blood pressure or joint problems.
  • Keep a sturdy chair or bench close for balance.
  • Breathe out as you push or lift. Never hold your breath.
  • Muscles working hard is good. Sharp pain, dizziness or chest pain – stop.
  • Slow and controlled beats fast. Lower for a count of 2–3.
  • Leave a day between workouts – muscles get stronger while they rest.
  • Start with a weight you can lift 10–15 times. Move up when that gets easy.
  • Warm up first: march on the spot or walk around the house for a few minutes.
  • Strength is half of it. The book also recommends about 30 minutes of walking or similar on most days.
