Go gentle – it's the point
  • Work at 30–50% of your max. Tension, never pain.
  • Jaw loose, breathe through your nose, never hold your breath.
  • If pressure builds in your head, skip that stretch or stop.
  • Forward folds: come up slowly, head last.
